\section{Introduction}\label{sec:future_work_introduction}

Future work for EduVerse focuses on two main directions: evaluating scalability
under broader real-world use and refining usability through structured user
feedback. These areas extend the current project by addressing aspects that were
not formally evaluated within the present testing scope.

\section{Scalability Improvements}\label{sec:scalability_improvements}

As EduVerse is used by larger numbers of organizations, users, classes, files,
and live sessions, future evaluation can examine how the platform performs
under heavier and more concurrent workloads. This would help establish practical
performance baselines and identify areas that may require further refinement as
usage grows.

Operational monitoring can also be strengthened to provide clearer information
about system behavior, resource demand, and service reliability during broader
use. Such evidence would allow future scalability decisions to be guided by
observed platform behavior rather than assumed limitations.

\section{User Feedback and Usability Refinement}
\label{sec:user_feedback_usability_refinement}

A second direction is broader evaluation with Organization Administrators,
Teachers, and Students. Structured feedback from these user groups can provide
evidence about navigation clarity, workflow understanding, feature
discoverability, and the overall usability of the integrated platform.

Findings from usability testing, questionnaires, or other structured feedback
methods can then guide targeted refinements to interfaces and workflows. The
objective is to preserve the integrated nature of EduVerse while keeping the
experience clear, efficient, and manageable as the platform evolves.

\section{Chapter Summary}\label{sec:future_work_summary}

Future development can strengthen EduVerse through empirical scalability
evaluation, improved operational monitoring, and user-informed usability
refinement.
